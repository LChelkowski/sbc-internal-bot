# SBC Cornell – Internal Member FAQ (Recruitment-Focused)

This knowledge base is for **internal SBC members** to guide consistent messaging during Fall 2025 recruitment.

---

## Applications & Process (internal notes)

**Q: When do applications open?**  
A: September 2, 2025.

**Q: When are applications due?**  
A: September 18, 2025 at 11:59 PM.

**Q: How should I direct someone to apply?**  
A: Tell them to go to [sbccornell.org/apply](https://www.sbccornell.org/apply).

**Q: What should I emphasize about applying?**  
A: Stress *early start, quality over quantity*, and polished resume + strong short answers.

**Q: How many interview rounds are there?**  
A: Two (Sept 20 and Sept 21, invite-only).

**Q: What do we tell candidates about interviews?**  
A: Round 1 = behavioral + problem-solving. Round 2 = case interview.

**Q: Should we disclose acceptance rates?**  
A: No exact numbers — frame it as “competitive, but we’re looking for genuine interest and fit.”

**Q: Do applicants need consulting experience?**  
A: No — highlight that SBC provides training through New Member Education (NME).

**Q: Do freshmen have a real chance?**  
A: Yes — emphasize that we welcome and train freshmen every semester.

**Q: What do we say if asked about reapplying?**  
A: Encourage them — many strong members were accepted on their 2nd or 3rd try.

## Coffee Chats (internal expectations)

**Q: How should members frame coffee chats?**  
A: Be professional, welcoming, and “sell the org” by highlighting social impact, real clients, and training.

**Q: Should members take notes during coffee chats?**  
A: No, keep it casual. Remember key points if follow-up is needed.

**Q: How long should a coffee chat last?**  
A: 20–30 minutes is standard.

**Q: What topics should we emphasize?**  
A: Projects, alumni success, mentorship, culture.

**Q: What should we avoid saying?**  
A: Don’t promise admission, don’t give exact acceptance rates, don’t speak negatively about other orgs.

**Q: Should members talk about their own experiences?**  
A: Yes — share personal growth, favorite projects, and the supportive culture.

## Member Conduct During Recruitment

**Q: Are members expected to attend all recruiting events?**  
A: Yes, attendance is required unless excused ahead of time. 

**Q: How should members interact with prospective applicants?**  
A: Be approachable, friendly, and professional. Make everyone feel welcome.

**Q: Should members discuss internal club issues with candidates?**  
A: No — keep the focus on positive, accurate messaging.

**Q: What if someone asks about application feedback?**  
A: Discuss this after recruitment; generally, we'll give applicants some feedback. 

**Q: What if an applicant asks about time commitment?**  
A: Tell them ~8–12 hours/week, but emphasize teamwork and manageable workload.

## Talking Points for Members

**Q: What differentiates SBC from other orgs?**  
A: Real (amazing) external clients, structured training, strong alumni, social impact focus.

**Q: What should we emphasize about alumni?**  
A: Alumni are in top consulting firms, grad schools, and startups; they mentor and recruit SBC members.

**Q: How should we frame the difficulty of cases?**  
A: Challenging but supportive — candidates will be trained.

**Q: What should we say about acceptance rates?**  
A: Competitive, but emphasize that passion for social impact and commitment matter most.

**Q: What should we emphasize about culture?**  
A: Collaborative, inclusive, tight-knit community, mentorship.

**Q: Should we talk about socials?**  
A: Yes, highlight that SBC members bond through retreats, dinners, and events. Be professional, i.e. don't mention getting wasted...

## Internal Do’s and Don’ts

**Q: What’s the #1 “do” during recruiting?**  
A: Represent SBC professionally and consistently.

**Q: What’s the #1 “don’t”?**  
A: Never badmouth other orgs, professors, or classmates.

**Q: What do I do if I don’t know the answer to a candidate’s question?**  
A: Direct them to the Ops team or the website.

**Q: Should members post about recruitment on social media?**  
A: Yes, make sure to repost on your instagram stories all information from the official SBC account.

**Q: Can members share internal timelines externally?**  
A: No — share only what is public-facing.

**Q: What should members do if they see misinformation spreading?**  
A: Politely correct it with accurate info from this FAQ or ask Ops.
